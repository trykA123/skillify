Method

6 fixtures, up to 3 runs per arm per task. 4 tasks are graded by hidden tests and 2 by a rubric graded by Codex. Each task runs in a throwaway repo, once with no skills and once with the task's skill loaded.

Results are read from evals/results in file-name order. When a later round re-ran a model, task and arm, the later round supersedes the earlier one (r2 supersedes r1). Means are per run. Cost is the CLI's own usage report, not an invoice.

Logged and published field runs update model cards and usage summaries. Field tokens and agent verdicts do not establish dollar costs or score improvements. Numeric comparisons require paired results; estimated costs are excluded from cost comparisons.
