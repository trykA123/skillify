# Review independence contract

For Heavy work, the reviewer must not be the implementation writer and must inspect the
same revision whose evidence is being claimed. If independence is unavailable, disclose
the limitation and do not approve.

For lighter work, self-review may provide evidence but never silently claims
independence. A named decision owner, not writer or reviewer status, accepts Material
risk.

Weight a result's own confidence inversely. Work that names its uncertainty is usually
the more accurate of the two; a clean self-assessment is the one to inspect first.
