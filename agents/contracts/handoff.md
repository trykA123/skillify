# Handoff contract

Every delegated result must let the receiver advance without reconstructing the run.
Return, in the lightest form the task weight permits:

- assigned outcome and boundary;
- selected Weight, Verbosity, Explanation, and exact ownership topology;
- result: completed, partial or blocked;
- artifacts or exact evidence locations;
- checks performed and their observed results;
- assumptions disproved, deviations and residual risks;
- unresolved decisions and the next valid owner or role.

Do not return raw internal narration, a success claim without evidence, or a question
that should have been escalated while work was still active. Light may keep this inline;
Standard and Heavy preserve it with the task's durable artifacts.
